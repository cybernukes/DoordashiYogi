A Journey Towards Health, Harmony, and Self-Realization

( Doordarshee Samooh )

Doordarshee Samooh is a holistic wellness initiative committed to spreading the ancient wisdom of Yog for physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. Rooted in the rich traditions of Indian yogic science, our mission is to integrate the timeless practices of Yog into modern lifestyles.

We believe Yog is not just a physical exercise—it is a way of life that leads to inner peace, balance, and self-awareness. Our organization is dedicated to making authentic Yog accessible to all, empowering individuals to live healthier, more conscious lives.

We don’t just teach Yog—we guide you on a path to inner peace, vibrant health, and conscious living.
